Legend of Varrowmere
A full 16-bit style action-adventure, playable right on Torn

Summary

Legend of Varrowmere is a top-down action-adventure in the style of the 16-bit classics, built for Torn. Explore a kingdom and its Shadow twin, fight through 19 dungeons, take on 15 side quests and bring down Chancellor Vex.

It's the first game in the new Legends folder on the Torn Arcade phone, and you can also open it from the Tampermonkey menu. It opens in its own window over the Torn page, makes no requests of any kind and needs no API key.

  • Two worlds: Varrowmere and Shadow Varrowmere, with towns, forests, snowy peaks, swamps, a desert and a long coast.
  • 19 dungeons: every one over several floors, with keys, puzzles, a mini-boss and a boss of its own.
  • 15 side quests: escorts, lost treasure, a wishing pond, hide and seek, a ghost's last wish and more.
  • Your own hero: three save files, each with its own name and tunic colour.
  • Leaderboards: Total playtime, Completion and Monsters defeated in Torn Arcade.

Features

A Proper Adventure

  • Win the Pendants, draw the Dawnblade, then follow Vex through the Twin Mirror into the Shadow world.
  • Find the Bow, Grapple, Power Glove, Twin Mirror, rods, wands, bottles, armour and shields, with a few Torn favourites: the Molotov Cocktail, Flash Grenade, Pepper Spray and Ninja Stars.
  • Shops, furnished houses, cellars, secret caves, fairy fountains and three Great Fairies.

Dungeons

  • 19 dungeons, each with its own floor plans, puzzles (crystal switches, dark rooms, pressure plates, water levels, push blocks), keys off the beaten path and a Big Key.
  • A different mini-boss and boss in every one.
  • Knocked out? You wake at the last floor you entered, not back at home.

Your Hero, Three Times

  • Three save files. Name each hero and pick one of five tunics. People in the game call you by your name.
  • Copy or erase files from the file select.

Items, Map and Leaderboards

  • A clear items screen with your gear, Pendants, Crystals, bottles, Heart Shards and side quests.
  • A full world map that shows where you are.
  • Total playtime, Completion and Monsters defeated go to the Torn Arcade leaderboards (the global boards use the Arcade's optional Online play).

How to Install

  1. Install Tampermonkey for your browser.
  2. Open the Legend of Varrowmere install link and click Install.
  3. Optional, but recommended: install the Soundtrack for the full music.
  4. Reload Torn and open the Legends folder on the Torn Arcade phone, or choose Play Legend of Varrowmere in the Tampermonkey menu.

It plays with a keyboard, so it's best on a PC or laptop. Updates install automatically through Tampermonkey, and your saves are kept.

Controls

  • Arrows / WASD: move. Z: sword (hold for a Spin Attack). X: use item. C: talk, lift, open.
  • Shift: dash (with the Running Shoes). Enter: items. M: map. N: music. Esc: close (it saves).

Scripting Rules

  • No requests: the game never contacts any website, Torn's API included. Everything is built into the script.
  • Never touches Torn's page: it doesn't read, click or change anything in Torn. It only opens its own window.
  • No automation: it's a game in a window, nothing more.
  • Open source to read: no outside websites, no websockets and no hidden code. The full source is on GitHub.
