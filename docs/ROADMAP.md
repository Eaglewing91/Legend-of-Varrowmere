# Legend of Varrowmere: roadmap to the full plan

The aim: a world 50% larger than the 1991 original, its full dungeon count, around 150 secrets, a full item set,
towns, side quests and minigames, with Torn-flavoured items and jokes. Light world first, then the Shadow side of each new region.

## Items still to add

Running Shoes (dash), Sledgehammer (pegs), Shovel (dig spots), Kazoo (call the bird: fast travel), Bug Net,
4 Bottles (potions, fairies), Book of Old Script, Frost Rod, three Medals (Quake, Flame, Storm), Brick Wand,
Riot Shield cane, Invisibility Cloak, Boxing Gloves (dark rocks), sword upgrades (smith, then the golden blade),
Blue and Red Mail, bomb and quiver upgrades, the energy meter used by rods, wands, medals and the cloak.

## Secrets (~150)

24 heart pieces in all, 4 bottles, 8 upgrade fountains, 30+ grottos and caves, dig spots, cash stashes,
bombable walls, mirror puzzles, and hidden rooms in every dungeon.

## Towns, people, side quests, minigames

Marrowgate (big town), Cinderfold, a desert camp, a mountain hermit, about 80 villagers.
Side quests: the smith's sword, the lost brother, the bottle merchant, the race. Minigames: shooting gallery,
chest game, digging field, the race, fishing.

## Torn flavour

Items and jokes from Torn (no Torn place names): Sledgehammer, Boxing Gloves, Kazoo, Riot Shield, energy drinks,
the hospital, mugging, nerve, the bazaar, plushies, and so on.

## Leaderboards (needs the Arcade server: an external site, so flagged and covered in the ToS)

Fastest completion, fewest deaths, most secrets.

Art pass, sound pass, balance, a full human playtest, then release through Torn Arcade.
